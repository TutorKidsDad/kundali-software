# Phase 7 — Shadbala diagnostic extension

## Added
- Drekkana Bala: 15 virupas in preferred decanate (Sun/Mars/Jupiter first; Mercury/Saturn second; Moon/Venus third), otherwise zero.
- Paksha Bala: based on exact Sun–Moon angular separation; waxing-benefic convention for Moon/Mercury/Jupiter/Venus, complement for Sun/Mars/Saturn. Mercury treatment and Moon classification vary by tradition.
- Both added to frontend and PDF diagnostics; total remains explicitly null.

## Still required before full Shadbala
- Saptavargaja Bala using verified varga dignity and compound friendships.
- Review Ojhayugma Mercury/Saturn convention.
- Replace whole-sign approximations for Dig/Kendradi with documented quadrant/house-angle methodology.
- Kala Bala: Natonnata, Tribhaga, Abda, Masa, Vara, Hora, Ayana, Yuddha (with solar times and local astronomical context).
- Cheshta Bala: validate the motional cycle and planetary mean-longitude/shighrocca conventions.
- Drik Bala: degree-sensitive aspect strength and benefic/malefic weighting, not whole-sign graha drishti.
- Independent numerical comparison against published worked examples (not yet performed).

**Do not present the partial subtotal as full Shadbala.**
